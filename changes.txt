FROG FC - changes.txt
Christopher Sapiens

Changes between the one-pager doc and the game I built.

CONTROLS:
	"Can't control the frog while you're in the air" - 
 		I built it like that, and the game didn't 
		work well. When two frogs landed on/next 
		to each other, they would get stuck. I 
		added some air controls so that you can 
		steer in the air.
  
	Everything else in Controls worked -
		Movement with arrows and WASD worked well 
		as well as the double jump with a weaker 
		second jump. I also included the tongue 
		shots with the shift buttons; the tongue 
		fires in whichever direction is held, with 
		a cooldown.

	Frogs land on each other -
 		Not in the doc. Frogs can land on each other 
		to block the other player's tongue.


HOLES:

	I said that if the ball is too fast, it would skip 
	the hole and not fall in. By using Discrete collision
	instead of continuous in the ball's Rigidbody, it 
	stops the ball from falling in every time.

	The frogs walking over the holes is done by adding a 
	solid collider above each hole, and by setting the 
	colliders to ignore the ball. This means frogs can 
	stand on it and balls fall in.


CHANGES NOT MENTIONED IN ONE-PAGER:

	Ball speed cap -
		When the ball was pinned against the wall, 
		it would force itself through the wall. I 
		capped the ball's speed and added a 0.25s 
		headbutt cooldown.

	Corner ramps -
		The ball kept getting stuck in the corners 
		of the arena and would reset, which was 
		annoying. I added angled ramps on each side 
		so the ball never gets stuck in the corner.

	Squeeze escape - 
		One big thing I noticed that I had to change
		was that players could ram the ball into 
		each other and the ball would get wedged in 
		between them. Then, it got really boring 
		because both players would just be pressing 
		the D and left arrow key to try and move each 
		other in the desired direction. I tried 
		friction, bounciness, and rounded colliders; 
		none of them worked. I changed the code to act 
		if a ball was basically still and touching a 
		player. In that case, it is lifted and dropped 
		back down. Now it gives the impression that a 
		ball wedged in between two players slips up 
		between them.

	Inactivity reset - 
		If the ball goes completely still for 15 seconds, 
		it respawns at the center. I made the timer 
		longer so that it doesn't punish someone who is 
		ahead/trying to protect their lead  


THE CHARGE BAR:

	Built from my maybe list -
		Three lily pad tiers, each one made active by a 
		tongue hit; each one makes the ball heavier and 
		slower.

	Position - 
		In the doc, I said it would be in the top right 
		corner. Instead, it's in the center to make it 
		easier to see.

	Decay timer - 
		Not in the doc. Because tongue hits happen so 
		often, the three tiers were hit immediately. 
		Those tiers stayed until someone scored, which 
		meant the ball was heavy all game. I made the 
		charge die down every few seconds, so the weight 
		of the ball is constantly changing. 


ART:

	Two different frogs -
		I said I would do one frog design with idle, 
		jumping, and tongue out frames. Instead, I drew 
		those frames for two different frog species (green 
		red-eyed tree frog and blue poison dart frog). 
		This made it easier to distinguish between players.

	No splash effect -
		While I added a splash sound effect, I did not
		add the visual effect of water splashing up.

	No frog crest title card - 
		I wanted to make a crest with frogs and their 
		tongues crossing. I kept my title card simpler for 
		time's sake.


AUDIO:

	Sourced from Freesound - 
		Didn't use Pixabay; found better sounds on Freesound.

	Headbutt thump cut -
		I was going to add a thump for headbutts, but along 
		with the other sound effects I added, there was too 
		much sound. Because headbutts happen so often, I 
		didn't want to run the risk of the audio being all 
		jumbled up.

	Tongue sound plays on the shot - 
		I was going to make the tongue make a sound when it 
		hit the ball, but instead made the sound when the 
		tongue was shot out.

	Frog impact sound cut - 
 		I was going to add a sound when frogs impacted each 
		other or were hit by each other's tongues, but I 
		decided not to.

	Charge beep cut - 
		I was going to add a sound when each charge tier was 
		added, but I decided not to.

	Croak pitch - 
		I added a croak for jumps and pitched the two players'
		croaks differently.


GAME FLOW:

	Title screen is an overlay - 
		I said the title screen would be its own thing, but 
		instead I decided to make it a screen over the game. 
		The overlay freezes the game until you hit Space. 

	Match win screen restarts the match - 
		Instead of going to the title screen, you are prompted 
		to press R to restart the match and play again. This way 
		you don't have to continue rereading the title screen/
		controls every time.
	
  	Kickoff, scorea, restart, and first-to-5 are all implemented as 
	mentioned in the doc.


MAYBES I CUT:

	Flies -
		Did not add the flies. I preferred to have the charge bar.
  		The flies would've been a whole separate system.

	Difficulty levels -
  		Wanted to add difficulty levels, didn't get to it. Maybe 
		I will add this later (different hole widths?).


CONCERNS REVISITED:

	"Getting a ball that is satisfying to hit will take the most 
	amount of time" - 
		This was true. It took a lot of time to get the gravity, 
		bouncing, damping, force, and speed cap right since they 
		all influence one another.
 		
	"Hole size is difficult" - 
		Made the hole size one unit wide. It's wide enough so that 
		the ball drops in if you place it, but narrow enough that 
		if you hit it too fast, it skips across it.

	"Scoring may be too hard: the tongue can save the ball" - 
 		Not really a problem. Scoring can be a little difficult. 
		Shooting your tongue to save the ball costs you the cooldown. 
		That being said, the save works, but it comes at a cost.

	"Need the two ways to hit the ball to be different" - 
		This one mattered the most, in my opinion. A headbutt is too 
		fast/strong, so it's pretty difficult to score with it. You 
		can clear with the headbutt. The togue makes it easier to place 
		the ball into the hole. 

	"If the tongue does not feel good after tuning, making the holes wider 
	makes the headbutt more useful than the tongue" -
		Luckily, I did not need this fallback. The holes are still 
		narrow and the tongue is still an important tool for scoring. 
		This was the version I wanted.

	I will add that at some point during my practice gameplay, I was 
	concerned about a player just camping/sitting over their goal so that 
	you cannot score. Because of the tongue feature, though, you can knock 
	players back and move them from their position, so this wasn't a big problem. 
	I will still try to find a way to fix this.
